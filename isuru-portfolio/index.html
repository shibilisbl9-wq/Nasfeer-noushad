01 — Profile

Hello, I'm Isuru

I'm a results-driven performance marketing specialist and creative strategist. I manage and scale paid campaigns across Meta, Google, TikTok and LinkedIn, with programmatic buying through Eskimi DSP and Oracle BlueKai.

Over the years I've worked with brands across e-commerce, real estate, higher education, SaaS and service businesses — most of them in the UAE and Sri Lanka, several of them scaled from a cold start.

I specialise in a “full-stack” performance approach. I don't just manage ad spend; I engineer the entire conversion journey — from the hook in the first three seconds to the attribution model that proves what the spend was worth.
